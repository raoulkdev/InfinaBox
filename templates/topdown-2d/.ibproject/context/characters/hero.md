# The hero

The character you play: a round sky-blue creature with a pink scarf whose
eyes look where it's walking. Waddles a little while moving, flashes red and
blinks when hurt. Has three hearts and can carry keys.

Look: `Visual` in `scenes/player.tscn` (body `#4fc3f7`, scarf `#ff7a8a`,
dark outline `#1e1b2e`). Movement and hearts: see `mechanics/movement.md`
and `mechanics/enemies-and-hearts.md`.
